# 💫 About Me:

I’m an **Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student 🎓** exploring the intersection of **embedded systems, IoT, cloud technologies, and AI** — with a growing focus on building intelligent software around real-world hardware and business problems.

🔧 **Hardware & IoT:** I work with **ESP32, ESP8266, ATmega328P, 8051, Arduino, sensors, MQTT, Mosquitto, and Home Assistant**, while building systems that connect physical devices with cloud platforms.

💻 **Software & Development:** I’m developing my programming foundation in **C/C++ and Python** while exploring **TypeScript, React, Next.js, REST APIs, databases, and modern full-stack development**.

🤖 **AI & Agentic Systems:** I’m particularly interested in **Generative AI, Agentic AI, prompt engineering, and AI-powered decision systems**. My ongoing **DEALER – AI Commerce Arena** project explores multi-agent negotiation, deterministic business policies, state machines, audit systems, and payment workflows.

☁️ **Cloud & Platforms:** I’m exploring **AWS, Google Cloud, Firebase, and cloud-connected IoT architectures**, with a focus on understanding how hardware, software, AI, and cloud services come together into scalable systems.

🚀 I enjoy learning by building — from **IoT and embedded projects** to **AI-powered applications and hackathon solutions**. I’ve also participated in programs and communities such as **GirlScript Summer of Code, Gen AI Academy, Prompt Wars, and Google Skills**, continuously expanding my technical toolkit.

My goal is to become an engineer who can **bridge hardware and software to build practical, intelligent, and scalable products.**
