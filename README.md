# 💫 About Me:
I am a second-year **Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering** student 🎓 dedicated to exploring the intersection of hardware and artificial intelligence. My technical journey ranges from low-level systems programming in **8051/8086 assembly** 📟 to developing sophisticated **IoT solutions** like CloudBME, a solar-powered environmental monitoring system ☀️. I specialize in bridging the gap between microcontrollers—such as the **ESP32 and ATmega328P**—and cloud platforms like AWS to build scalable, real-world applications ☁️.<br><br>Beyond the hardware stack, I am deeply invested in the world of **Agentic AI** and **Prompt Engineering** 🤖. With certifications from IBM and my ongoing work building **TechNova**—a startup focused on AI-driven services—I aim to turn complex generative tools into practical business solutions 🚀. Whether I am optimizing **VLSI signal processing** algorithms, contributing to **GirlScript Summer of Code** 💻, or exploring the artisanal world of niche fragrances 🧪, I am driven by a passion for continuous learning and technical precision.
